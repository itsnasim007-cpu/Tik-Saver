Tik Saver — Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Tik Saver is an independent project by MD M Nasim. This starter website does not require account registration.

Information and links

When you submit a TikTok URL, the server processes that URL to request public embed metadata from TikTok's public oEmbed service. The app does not intentionally request private account data.

Clipboard

The Paste button reads clipboard text only after you tap it and grant browser permission. You can also paste manually.

Advertising

Advertising integrations are not enabled in this starter package. If ad providers are added later, update this policy to describe their data collection, cookies, and consent requirements.

Downloads

Direct video downloads are disabled until an authorized provider is configured. Only save content you own or have permission to download.
